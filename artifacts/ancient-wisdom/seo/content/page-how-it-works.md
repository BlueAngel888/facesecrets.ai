## 1. You add a portrait

Upload a JPG, PNG or GIF up to 16 MB, or take a photo with your camera. The best results come from one person, facing the camera, in soft even light, with no sunglasses or filters.

## 2. The photo is checked

The server first checks that the image contains one clear, front-facing face. If it does not, the session stops and the photo is deleted.

## 3. AI reads the face, zone by zone

The image is analysed by an AI vision model, which describes only what is visible in 12 zones:

| Zone | What is described |
| --- | --- |
| Forehead | Height, width, lines |
| Eyebrows | Shape, thickness, position |
| Eyes | Shape, spacing, expression |
| Nose | Bridge, tip, proportions |
| Mouth and lips | Fullness, width, corners |
| Chin and jaw | Width, angle, definition |
| Cheekbones | Prominence |
| Ears | Visible size and position |
| Skin | Visible texture and tone, as photographed |
| Lines | Expression lines |
| Face shape | Overall outline |
| Symmetry | Balance between left and right |

The report also rates image quality (lighting, pose, occlusions) and states how confident it is.

## 4. Five traditions, side by side

Each observation is then reflected through five lenses: Chinese Mian Xiang, Greek physiognomy, Vedic Samudrika and Ayurveda, Islamic Firasa, and modern morphopsychology and FACS/Ekman expression science. They are presented as cultural perspectives for self-reflection, never as facts or predictions.

## 5. You download your report

You receive a PDF with an executive summary, the zone-by-zone reading, the five perspectives, personal development prompts and five long-term practices. Reports are available in English, Romanian, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic and Japanese.

## What FaceSecrets does not do

- It does not identify you or compare your face with other faces.
- It does not assess health, intelligence, ethnicity, sexuality or other sensitive traits.
- It does not keep your photo: it is deleted as soon as the analysis finishes. Read the [privacy policy](/privacy).
