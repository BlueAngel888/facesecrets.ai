## How the symmetry test works

1. The photo is cropped to a portrait frame on your device.
2. The test searches for the vertical midline where the two halves of the image match best. You can adjust it, and correct head tilt.
3. It builds a **left-left** face (the left half plus its mirror) and a **right-right** face.
4. It compares every pixel on one side with its mirror on the other side and turns the average difference into a score from 0 to 100.

## How to get a fair result

- Face the camera directly, with your head level.
- Use soft, even light from the front; side light creates shadows that read as asymmetry.
- Hold the camera at least an arm's length away to reduce lens distortion.
- Keep a neutral expression and pull hair away from your face.

## What the score means

The score is an estimate of how similar the two halves of the **photo** are. It changes with lighting, pose and hair, so compare photos taken the same way. It is not a measure of beauty, health or personality. Read the [facial symmetry guide](/guides/facial-symmetry) to learn what symmetry does and does not mean.
