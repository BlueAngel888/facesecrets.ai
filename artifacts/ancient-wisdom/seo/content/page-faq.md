## About the service

### What is FaceSecrets?

FaceSecrets is an online AI face reading service. You upload one portrait and receive a private PDF report describing visible features in 12 facial zones, symmetry and expression, interpreted through five face-reading traditions.

### How does AI face reading work?

An AI vision model describes what is visible in your photo zone by zone. Those observations are then reflected through Chinese Mian Xiang, Greek physiognomy, Vedic Samudrika, Islamic Firasa and modern expression science. See [how it works](/how-it-works).

### How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes from upload to download.

### What photo works best?

One person, facing the camera, in soft and even light, without sunglasses, heavy filters or other faces in the picture.

## Price and languages

### Which languages are supported?

English, Romanian, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic and Japanese.

### Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up; the report is tied to your browser session.

## Privacy

### What happens to my photo?

The photo is used only to create your report and is deleted as soon as the analysis finishes or fails. The report is deleted from the server once you download it. Read the [privacy policy](/privacy).

### Does FaceSecrets recognise or identify faces?

No. It does not identify people, create face templates or compare your face with other faces.

## Accuracy

### Is face reading scientific?

No. Physiognomy and similar traditions are not scientifically validated ways to read personality or predict the future. Facial symmetry and expressions can be measured, but they do not reveal character. FaceSecrets presents traditional readings as prompts for reflection.

### Can a photo show microexpressions?

Not really. Microexpressions last a fraction of a second and need video. A photo shows the resting expression and expression lines. See the [microexpressions guide](/guides/microexpressions).

### Is there a free option?

Yes: the [free face symmetry test](/tools/face-symmetry-test) runs in your browser and never uploads your photo.
