## How to find your face shape

1. Pull your hair back and stand in front of a mirror, or take a straight-on photo at arm's length or further.
2. Measure **face length** from the hairline to the bottom of the chin.
3. Measure the width of the **forehead**, the **cheekbones** and the **jawline**.
4. Note the shape of the jaw: rounded, angular or pointed.

## The 7 face shapes

| Shape | Proportions | Jaw | Five-element reading (Chinese face reading) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oval | Length about 1.5 times width; forehead slightly wider than jaw | Softly rounded | Metal/earth balance: steady, adaptable |
| Round | Length and width similar; full cheeks | Rounded | Water: sociable, flexible, emotional depth |
| Square | Forehead, cheekbones and jaw similar in width | Strong, angular | Earth: practical, reliable, determined |
| Oblong (rectangle) | Clearly longer than wide; straight sides | Angular or rounded | Wood: growth, idealism, persistence |
| Heart | Wide forehead, narrow chin | Pointed | Fire (inverted): imaginative, intense |
| Diamond | Cheekbones widest; narrow forehead and jaw | Pointed | Fire/metal: focused, independent |
| Triangle | Jaw wider than forehead | Broad | Earth: grounded, endurance |

The element readings are traditional and symbolic. They are useful for reflection, not as personality facts.

## Why your face shape can look different in photos

Camera distance changes proportions: a phone held close makes the center of the face larger and the jaw narrower. Hair, beards and posture also change the outline. Measure from a photo taken from at least one metre away.
