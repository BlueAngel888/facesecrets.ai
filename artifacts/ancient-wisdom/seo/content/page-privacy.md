## What we collect

- **Your portrait photo**, only when you choose to upload or take one for analysis.
- **Your selected language** and the report generated for you.
- **A session cookie**, so your report can be delivered to the browser that requested it.
- **Payment records.** Web payments are processed by Stripe. We never receive your card details.

We do not ask for your name or email to create a report, and we do not use your data for advertising.

## How your photo is used

Your photo is used only to create the report you requested. Our server checks that the image contains one clear, front-facing face, then sends it to our AI provider, OpenAI, which generates the report text on our behalf. We do not create face templates, do not identify you and do not compare your face with other faces.

## How long we keep it

- Your photo is deleted from our server as soon as the analysis finishes or fails, or when you cancel.
- Your report and its PDF are deleted from our server as soon as you download them.
- Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting, fraud prevention and refunds.

## Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run FaceSecrets: hosting (Replit), AI analysis (OpenAI) and payments (Stripe). We do not sell personal data.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR, you may ask to access or delete your data, or object to its processing.

## Important context

Reports draw on historical, non-scientific face-reading traditions and are for entertainment and self-reflection only. They are not medical, psychological, financial or predictive advice.

## Children

FaceSecrets is not intended for anyone under 16.
